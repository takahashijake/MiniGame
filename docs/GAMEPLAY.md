# Gameplay Guide

## Main commands

- W - walk and trigger an exploration event.
- I - inspect inventory.
- M - visit the travelling merchant.
- B - approach the boss gate.
- Q - quit.

## Combat commands

- A - attack.
- H - consume one Potion and restore HP.
- R - attempt to flee.

Knights are durable physical enemies. Mages have less health but stronger healing. Normal enemies can occasionally retreat. The Dragon never retreats.

## Economy and progression

Normal victories award 2-5 Gold and can also drop a Potion. Exploration can reveal Potions, a Sword, a Key, or Gold.

Merchant prices:

- Potion: 3 Gold
- Sword: 8 Gold
- Key: 6 Gold

The Sword is permanent and adds 8 damage to every player attack. The Key is consumed when the boss gate is first opened. The gate remains open afterward.

To challenge the Dragon, win at least three normal battles and open the gate with a Key. Defeating the Dragon completes the run.
